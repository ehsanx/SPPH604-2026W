# Labs L1–L6

Six in-person labs, Thursdays 10 am–12 pm in **SPPH 143**, Weeks 2–7.

Each lab teaches one method on a fixed teaching dataset. The matching **practice
increment** then applies that same method to your own locked paper — see
[`practice/`](https://ehsanx.github.io/SPPH604-2026W/practice/README.html).

| Lab | Topic | Feeds | Due |
|---|---|:--:|---|
| [**L0**](L0_setup.md) | **Setup — do before Week 1** | — | *self-study, not graded* |
| [L1](L1_data_wrangling.md) | NHANES data wrangling | — | Thu Sept 17 |
| [L2](L2_nhanes_mortality.md) | NHANES–mortality linkage | P1 | Thu Sept 24 |
| [L3](L3_confounding.md) | Confounding & collapsibility | P2 | Thu Oct 1 |
| [L4](L4_interaction.md) | Effect modification & interaction | P3 | Thu Oct 8 |
| [L5](L5_survey_analysis.md) | Complex survey analysis | P4 | Thu Oct 15 |
| [L6](L6_missing_data.md) | Missing data & multiple imputation | P5 | Thu Oct 22 |

All six work the same way: the TA explains the worked solution, you run it yourself, you
knit it, you submit the knitted file before the session ends. **Graded Complete /
Incomplete**, 10% of the course between them. Bring a laptop with R installed.

Submissions go through Canvas.
